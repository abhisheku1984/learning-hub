# THE LEARNING HUB — AI PROMPT PACK
### 16 work-ready prompts for work, career and content
Free resource from The Learning Hub — https://abhisheku1984.github.io/learning-hub/

How to use: copy a prompt, replace the [brackets] with your details, paste into your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.), then refine the output.

---

## 1. Meeting to actions (WORK)
**When to use:** After any meeting
> Turn these notes into decisions, owners and deadlines. Notes: [paste notes]
**Example input:** Raw bullet notes
**Expected output:** Table of actions with owner and date

## 2. Career roadmap (CAREER)
**When to use:** Planning your next move
> I am a [role] with [skills]. Build a 12-month roadmap to become [target role], with monthly goals and projects.
**Example input:** Analyst → AI product lead
**Expected output:** 12-month plan with milestones

## 3. Resume bullet rewriter (RESUME)
**When to use:** Weak bullets
> Rewrite this bullet with action verb, scope and measurable result. Do not invent numbers: [bullet]
**Example input:** Managed reports weekly
**Expected output:** Strong bullet with metric placeholders

## 4. LinkedIn headline (LINKEDIN)
**When to use:** Profile refresh
> Write 5 LinkedIn headlines for a [role] who helps [audience] with [skill].
**Example input:** Analyst, operations teams, forecasting
**Expected output:** Five options under 220 characters

## 5. Ad copy variants (MARKETING)
**When to use:** Launching a campaign
> Write 5 ad headlines and 3 descriptions for [product] aimed at [audience]. Tone: [tone].
**Example input:** Online course for managers
**Expected output:** Headline and copy variants

## 6. Excel formula helper (EXCEL)
**When to use:** Stuck on a formula
> I have columns A:[x]. Write an Excel formula to [goal] and explain it.
**Example input:** Sum sales by region
**Expected output:** Formula plus short explanation

## 7. Power BI measure (POWER BI)
**When to use:** Writing DAX
> Write a DAX measure for [goal] using tables [names]. Explain each part.
**Example input:** Year-on-year growth
**Expected output:** DAX code with explanation

## 8. Presentation outline (PRESENTATIONS)
**When to use:** Before building slides
> Create a 7-slide outline for [audience] about [topic] with one key message per slide.
**Example input:** Quarterly review
**Expected output:** Slide titles with messages

## 9. Research brief (RESEARCH)
**When to use:** New topic
> Summarise [topic] in 5 key points, 3 open questions and sources I should verify.
**Example input:** Workforce planning with AI
**Expected output:** Short structured brief

## 10. Code explainer (CODING)
**When to use:** Reading unfamiliar code
> Explain this code line by line for a beginner, then suggest 3 improvements: [code]
**Example input:** A Python function
**Expected output:** Plain explanation and tips

## 11. Business case (BUSINESS)
**When to use:** Pitching an idea
> Write a one-page business case for [idea]: problem, solution, cost, benefit, risks.
**Example input:** Automating weekly reports
**Expected output:** One-page structured case

## 12. Daily planner (PRODUCTIVITY)
**When to use:** Start of day
> Here are my tasks and deadlines: [list]. Prioritise them and build a time-blocked plan.
**Example input:** 8 tasks, 6 hours
**Expected output:** Prioritised schedule

## 13. Content calendar (CONTENT)
**When to use:** Weekly posting
> Plan 5 LinkedIn posts on [theme] with hook, body idea and call to action.
**Example input:** AI for managers
**Expected output:** Five post plans

## 14. Video script (VIDEO)
**When to use:** Making a short video
> Write a 60-second script on [topic] with hook, 3 points and call to action. Add scene notes.
**Example input:** 3 prompt tips
**Expected output:** Timed script with scenes

## 15. Image prompt (IMAGE)
**When to use:** Creating visuals
> Write an image prompt for [subject] with style, lighting, camera angle and mood.
**Example input:** Futuristic office, gold light
**Expected output:** Detailed prompt

## 16. Lesson planner (EDUCATION)
**When to use:** Teaching a topic
> Build a 30 minute lesson on [topic] with goal, activity and quiz.
**Example input:** Prompt basics
**Expected output:** Timed lesson plan
