Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Your resume never leaves your device

The Resume + LinkedIn Optimizer runs entirely in your browser using local keyword matching. The text you paste is not uploaded, stored, or sent to any server. Closing the tab removes it.

What this site stores locally

This site uses your browser's local storage for two small preferences only: whether the intro film has been seen this session, and your sound preference. These contain no personal data and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic (page views, country, device type). It does not use cookies for this and does not track individual visitors across other websites.

Downloads

Free resources (prompt pack, tool guide, checklist) download directly from this site. No email address or personal data is required.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact details will be published here soon. Until then, you can reach the project via its GitHub repository.
